# Explanation

Background that helps you understand {{title}}: the ideas behind it, the
reasons for its design and the trade-offs that were made.

Explanation is for reading away from the keyboard. It can discuss alternatives,
history and opinion, and it connects things. It does not give steps or lists of
facts; it links to the how-to guides and the reference for those.

## What belongs here

- The big picture: how the parts fit together.
- Why a design choice was made, and what it costs.
- A comparison with other approaches.
