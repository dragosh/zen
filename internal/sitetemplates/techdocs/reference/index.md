# Reference

Exact, complete and neutral descriptions of the parts of {{title}}: commands,
settings, names, limits and defaults.

Reference is for looking things up, not for reading from top to bottom. It
follows the shape of the thing it describes, uses the same layout on every page,
and says what is true without advice or opinion. Examples are short and exist to
show a form, not to teach.

## What belongs here

- Tables of options, with their types and defaults.
- Command lines and their flags.
- Limits, error messages and their meaning.

## Pages in this site

- [Settings](settings.md): an example reference page that shows the shape.
