# How-to guides

Steps to get one real job done. The reader already knows the basics and has a
goal in mind.

A how-to guide is a recipe: a title that names the goal, then the steps, and
nothing else. It does not teach, and it does not explain background; it links
to the explanation instead. The guide may assume some knowledge, and it can
cover a few variations of the task.

## What belongs here

- A title that starts with "How to" and names a goal.
- Numbered steps that lead to that goal.
- Warnings about the usual mistakes, at the step where they happen.
