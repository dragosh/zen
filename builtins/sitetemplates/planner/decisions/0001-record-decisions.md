## Context

Plans change, and people forget why a choice was made. New people ask the same
questions again, and old arguments return.

## Decision

We keep one short page for each important decision, in this folder. The file
name starts with a number, then a short name. Use the [template](template.md).

## Consequences

- Decisions are easy to find and to question later.
- Writing one takes a few minutes, which slows us down a little.
- A decision can be replaced: add a new record that says so, and change the old status to "replaced".
