# Glossary

The words used in the paths, in plain language. Keep the list in alphabetical
order, and link to a word from the step where it first appears.

## C

**Cue**
: The moment that tells you to start a routine, such as finishing a meal.

## H

**Habit**
: An action that you repeat at the same time and place until it needs little effort.

## R

**Reward**
: Something that feels good right after a routine and makes you want to repeat it.

**Routine**
: The small action you repeat. Keep it so small that you can always do it.
