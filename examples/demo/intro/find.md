# Find in a page

Press <kbd>⌘F</kbd>, click the magnifying glass in the top bar, or choose
**Edit › Find…**, and a small find bar opens at
the top right of the window. Type, and every match on the page is marked; the
current one stands out and scrolls into view.

| Keys | Action |
|---|---|
| <kbd>⌘F</kbd> | open the find bar, or select its text when it is open |
| <kbd>⌘G</kbd> or <kbd>↩</kbd> | the next match |
| <kbd>⇧⌘G</kbd> or <kbd>⇧↩</kbd> | the previous match |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | close the bar |

The bar shows where you are, such as **3 of 12**, and wraps around at the end.
**Aa** turns on match case. What you type is plain text: a `.` or a `*` is
looked for as it is.

## Try it

Press <kbd>⌘F</kbd> and type *zenmark*: the bar counts every match on this
page, whatever its case. Now press **Aa** and the count drops to the matches
written exactly as you typed them. ZENMARK, Zenmark and zenmark are three
spellings to try.

Find looks at the page's own text, including text inside plugins such as code
blocks and checklists. The marks never change the page, and they go away when
you close the bar or open another page.
