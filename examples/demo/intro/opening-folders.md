# Open a folder

Zenmark works on **one folder** per window. Everything it shows stays inside
that folder.

## How to open one

- Click **Open a folder** on the welcome screen.
- Or choose **File › Open Folder…**, or press <kbd>⌘O</kbd>.
- **File › Close Folder** (<kbd>⇧⌘W</kbd>) goes back to the welcome screen.

## What Zenmark shows first

If the folder has a `README.md` at its root, Zenmark opens it. Otherwise pick a
page in the sidebar. This guide opens its own `README.md`.

## Very large folders

Zenmark refuses folders with more than **2,000 markdown files** or **50,000
files and folders**, so opening your home folder by accident is harmless.
While a folder opens, a loader shows what is happening, with a **Cancel**
button.
