# The article layout

`article` is the reading view: a narrow serif column, the same one you are
reading now. Every page without a layout of its own uses it, and it adds
nothing around the page.

## Use it

You never need to ask for it. To say so anyway:

```yaml
---
title: My page
layout: article
---
```

## What it reads from a page

Nothing beyond the usual keys, like `title`; see
[frontmatter](../intro/frontmatter.md). It has no options of its own.

## Make it easier to read

The shape of the text is yours to adjust. In **Settings › Accessibility** you
can change the text size, font, line spacing and line width, and zoom the
whole window with <kbd>⌘+</kbd> and <kbd>⌘-</kbd>.
