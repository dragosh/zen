A **plugin** is a part of a page: a diagram, a callout, a quiz. A **layout** is
the look and feel of the whole page: its structure, type and behaviour. A page
chooses one in its frontmatter, and a folder can choose by path.

```yaml
---
layout: docs
---
```

This page uses the `landing` layout, and the cards below are the pages under
it. Each one shows its layout at work. Settings has a Layouts section that
lists them with help and snippets to copy.
